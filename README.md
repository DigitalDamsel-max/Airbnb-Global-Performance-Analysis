# Airbnb Global Performance Analysis


## 1. Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Airbnb’s global performance across listings, cities, property types, reviews, ratings, pricing, host verification, and seasonal trends.
The project converts Airbnb data into business insights to understand market growth, city-level performance, customer review behavior, and host trust signals.


## 2. Key Metrics
- 279,712 total listings
- 10 cities analyzed
- 182,024 hosts
- 144 property types
- 5.373 million reviews


## 3. Key Findings
1. **Paris, New York, and Sydney** account for almost half of the total listings and **59% of total reviews**.
2. **Paris** has the highest number of listings and reviews among the analyzed cities.
3. **Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro** have the highest overall ratings, while Hong Kong and Istanbul have comparatively lower ratings.
4. **Cleanliness and value for money** are the lowest-scoring rating dimensions across cities.
5. **98.8% of reviewers** submitted reviews three times or fewer, indicating that most customers reviewed only once or a limited number of times.
6. Paris and Rome show the highest review activity from **April to August**, reflecting the European summer travel season.
7. More than two-thirds of Airbnb hosts are fully verified, providing a strong trust signal for customers.
8. Airbnb reached its highest number of new listings in **2015**, followed by slower growth during 2016–2017 and disruption during the COVID-19 period.
